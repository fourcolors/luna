Constellation · the full life of a star

The governing idea: the constellation is the present tense, the conversation is the past tense. Stars are born from the moon when Luna spawns work, hang while it runs (thread length = how long it's been running), turn gold when blocked on you, and when finished they fall into the conversation as a result message. Nothing lingers in the sky that isn't live — history belongs to the chat thread. Each card below covers one moment of actually living with it.

1 The lifecycle

Every star follows the same five-beat arc — this is the grammar everything else obeys.

  • Born: when a job spawns (by you, by schedule, by the wake reasoner), its star drops from the moon on a thread
  • Working: the thread slowly lengthens — an old star hanging low looks long-running, no numbers needed
  • Done: the star falls out of the sky; the result becomes a message in the conversation. The sky only ever shows now.

3 Pluck

The re-tether string mechanic, inverted: pull a star down to harvest it.

  • A short tug stretches the thread and springs back — safe to fidget
  • Pull past the threshold and release → the star opens (here: a side panel; in the widget: the chat, focused on that agent)
  • Deliberate-by-design: you can't open one by accident, unlike a click

4 Living margin · chat open

No pill shelf, no extra chrome — the constellation IS the tab bar.

  • Click a star → its run timeline slides over the messages (the star brightens = active tab); "← conversation" or the moon brings you home
  • The ask arrives twice, answered once: the gold star AND a normal message from Luna in the conversation. Answer from either — they're the same question. You never have to notice the constellation to not miss it.
  • Completion: the star falls and the result lands as a message — try the fast-forward. The conversation is the system of record.
