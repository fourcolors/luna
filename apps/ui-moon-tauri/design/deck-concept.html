Where notifications live

Three intensities of the same gold: the mote turns gold (peripheral vision), the pip on the moon goes gold (count + urgency at a glance), and a toast slides out beside the moon naming the asker. risk_tier decides how far up that ladder a given ask climbs — a Tier 0 question might only gild the mote.

Continuity is the trick

The mote you watched orbit while minimized is the same object you act on in the deck — it flies into the rail when the deck assembles. Nothing teleports; the notification you saw IS the row you click IS the workflow you answer.

And it's still a deck

Everything from the previous round survives: drag widgets by their title bars (magnet snap, ghost preview), the briefing folds when you focus the input, workflows open inspectors, agents open direct lines. Close every widget → pure chat.
