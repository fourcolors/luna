Different things, different windows

Your ontology decides the widget: a workflow is a pipeline, so its widget is an inspector you look at. An agent is a colleague, so its widget is a direct line you talk to — its own input, its own bubbles, no impersonating Luna. Cron stays a heartbeat in the stream. Nothing pretends to be something it isn't.

The chat stays pure

Nothing is baked into the conversation surface — the rail, the board, the inspector are all removable. Close every widget and you have exactly the moon chat you ship today. The deck is progressive disclosure with physical metaphors instead of menus.

The briefing yields to you

Like your screenshot: status up top when you arrive — but the moment you focus the input, it folds to one slim line. Your intent always outranks ambient status. Click the slim line to get it back.
