Luna is tethered

          
Your moon has a home.

          
          
Luna is awake, but she hasn’t met Claude yet. In the Terminal app run claude setup-token, copy the token it prints, and add this line to ~/.luna/.env on Luna’s machine:
CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN=…the token…
Then restart Luna (re-run this wizard and pick “This Mac” → Update) and she’s ready to chat.

          
Your moon keeps itself up to date on its own — and any time Luna herself needs a refresh, just re-run this wizard from Settings and pick “This Mac” again.
